Graduate Work

My thesis work will focus on designing and prototyping a secure data architecture for the University of Arkansas' National Multi-User Silicon Carbide Research and Fabrication Laboratory (MUSiC). The architecture is meant to support future AI/ML training and optimization while maintaining cybersecurity requirements.

My goal is to contribute to the foundation of the facility's data pipeline by putting in place server infrastructure, implementing SBC data translators for legacy equipment, setting up a centralized historian, and designing a secure path for data flow into a data lake.
